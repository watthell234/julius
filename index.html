Growing up between two languages and two cultures taught me to adapt quickly, listen closely, and feel at home somewhere new. I still lean on that every day.

My route to a four-year degree started at community college. Some people see that as a detour. For me it was a launchpad. I put my head down, excelled, and earned my transfer to Binghamton University.

Binghamton University · B.S. Financial Economics · 2015

Where I fell for startups

I studied Financial Economics, but entrepreneurship is what grabbed me. A small group of people with an idea and enough grit could build something from nothing and put it in people's hands. I went to every talk I could find and started treating problems as things to build.

The first startup

The AR magic book

My first real startup was an augmented reality magic book: a printed book that came alive when you pointed a phone at it. Characters stepped off the page, and kids could explore a story instead of only reading it.

Partway through the build, my technical cofounder quit.

Nobody was left to build it, so I decided to become someone who could. I taught myself to code, one tutorial, one bug and one late night at a time.

The lesson I still live by: when you're blocked, learn your way through it.
